About

Hard questions, tested honestly

Two Tail Technologies builds data analytics, modeling and AI tools, and holds every one of them to a research-grade standard of testing. It was founded in 2026 by twins Aaron and Mark Scott.

Method

How we work

We start with the problem, not the technique. Before building anything, we define the simplest reasonable answer, and every new tool has to beat it.

01

Beat the simple answer

Every tool is measured against the rule of thumb it would replace. It has to beat that baseline before we count it as progress.

02

Test on unseen data

Every tool is scored on data it never saw during development. That is the score that shows how it will perform in practice.

03

Pass more than once

One good run can be luck. A result has to hold up across repeated runs, and every project must rebuild from a fresh copy and reproduce it, so anyone can verify the work.

04

Report what we find

A documented null result is still a result. A win that holds only under perfect conditions is not one we will claim.

05

Improve in small steps

We work in small cycles, borrowed from the Toyota Production System: propose an idea, test it, keep what works and fix what does not. Each round sets up the next.

What we work on

Our work so far spans a colony-survival game, economic planning, financial markets, company forecasting, land development, business operations, labor policy, political finance, affordable housing, armed conflict and the wiring of the fruit fly brain. Each project page states plainly where the work stands, including projects that are still ideas. See our products and research pipeline.
