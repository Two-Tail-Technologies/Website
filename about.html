About

Hard questions, tested honestly

Two Tail Technologies is an applied research venture founded in November 2026 by twins Aaron and Mark Scott. We work on questions worth answering well: how an economy could be planned, how markets move, how a business runs day to day and how a brain is wired.

How we work

We start with the problem, not the technique. Before building anything, we define the simplest reasonable answer, and every new tool has to beat it.

Every tool is scored on data it never saw during development, and every test is run more than once. A result that appears in one run and disappears in the next is not yet a result. Each project must rebuild from a fresh copy and reproduce its numbers, so anyone can verify the work.

We improve in small, deliberate steps, the continuous-improvement discipline of the Toyota Production System. Each project moves from idea to test to revision, and every round of results shapes the next.

We report what we find, including when an idea does not work. A documented null result is useful. A win that holds only under perfect conditions is not one we will claim.

What we work on

Our work so far spans a colony-survival game, economic planning, financial markets, company forecasting, land development, business operations, labor policy, political finance, affordable housing, armed conflict and the wiring of the fruit fly brain. Each project page states plainly where the work stands, including projects that are still ideas.
