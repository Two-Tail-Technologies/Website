Conflict Forecasting

Can news-event data improve forecasts of armed conflict deaths, especially in countries where fighting has not yet begun?

python · forecasting · event data

The question

Do monthly country features built from the GDELT news-event database improve out-of-sample forecasts of state-based conflict deaths over the published VIEWS benchmark models, particularly for new outbreaks in countries with little recent violence? It is a prediction question and makes no claim about why conflicts start.

Where it stands

The research plan is drafted, and the data licenses have been reviewed: conflict data from the Uppsala Conflict Data Program and news-event data from GDELT. Nothing is built yet.

How it will be tested

The VIEWS benchmarks are reproduced first, alongside a simple regression on past conflict. The model is trained on conflict history alone, then on history plus news data; the difference between the two is the answer. Forecasts are scored with rolling evaluation that never shuffles time, using proper probabilistic scores.

The strongest test comes last: freeze the model, publish its forecasts and score them as new conflict data arrives.
